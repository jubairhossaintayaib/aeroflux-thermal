A clear quotation and the right questions can help you prepare for a heating or cooling investment.

Start with a complete quotation

Know what you are paying for before making a decision. Your proposal should describe the equipment, installation work, exclusions, applicable VAT and payment schedule.

Explore Scottish home-energy support

Home Energy Scotland provides information about grants and loans for eligible clean heating and energy-efficiency improvements. Check the latest eligibility, approval process and installer requirements directly with the scheme.

Explore the Home Energy Scotland Grant and Loan

Questions worth asking

  • Is the quoted price fixed, and what could change it?
  • Are surveys, electrical work and making good included?
  • When are payments due, and what are the cancellation terms?
  • Does funding require approval before the work starts?
  • Which installer and product requirements apply?
AeroFlux has no confirmed consumer-credit or finance arrangement for this preview. No lending, credit decision, guaranteed grant or monthly payment offer is made here.
