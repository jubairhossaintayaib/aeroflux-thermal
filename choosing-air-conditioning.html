Choosing air conditioning for a room you love

Comfort comes from the whole design: capacity, positioning, noise, controls and practical installation details.

AeroFlux home comfort guide · 4 October 2026 · 3 min read

Describe how you use the room

A bedroom, home office and sunlit living room can have different cooling needs. Tell the installer about the room’s size, windows, shade, occupancy and the hours it is used. A site assessment is more useful than a blanket recommendation.

Discuss the indoor and outdoor positions

Consider the direction of airflow, maintenance access, drainage, pipe routes and where an outdoor unit can sit. Ask how noise will be assessed and what the installation will look like when finished.

Compare complete quotations

Check that equipment, installation work, electrical provisions, making good and commissioning are clear. Understand any exclusions and the conditions attached to the product warranty.

Plan for maintenance

Ask how to clean accessible filters safely and when professional servicing is needed. For work involving F-gas refrigerants, verify the relevant engineer qualifications and company certification.
