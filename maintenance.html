Give your equipment a considered maintenance routine, with checks and advice that match the system in your property.

Plan around your equipment

A maintenance plan should explain what is covered, how often visits happen and what is excluded. A household boiler, a heat pump and a busy commercial cooling system can have different needs.

A clear service scope

  • Equipment identification and a review of service history.
  • Checks appropriate to the appliance and its manufacturer’s instructions.
  • Accessible cleaning and adjustments where included.
  • A written record of findings and suggested follow-up.
  • Practical advice on controls and routine user maintenance.

Maintenance questions

How often should I have my boiler or HVAC system serviced?

Arrange an annual service for gas appliances with a suitably qualified Gas Safe registered engineer. Heat pumps and air conditioning should follow the manufacturer’s service schedule; usage and the building can affect the interval. A routine check helps spot wear before a breakdown.

HSE gas safety guidance

Who should install or repair my air conditioning?

Use an appropriately qualified contractor. Work involving fluorinated refrigerants requires the relevant F-gas qualifications and, where applicable, company certification. Ask for the credentials relevant to your equipment before agreeing to the work.

GOV.UK F-gas guidance

Can I get a price before any work starts?

A useful quotation should separate the visit or diagnostic charge, recommended work, parts and applicable VAT. Some faults need an on-site inspection first. Ask what is included, which options are available and how additional work would be agreed.
