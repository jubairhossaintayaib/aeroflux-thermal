How often should I have my boiler or HVAC system serviced?

Arrange an annual service for gas appliances with a suitably qualified Gas Safe registered engineer. Heat pumps and air conditioning should follow the manufacturer’s service schedule; usage and the building can affect the interval. A routine check helps spot wear before a breakdown.

HSE gas safety guidance

Will a heat pump keep my home warm in a Scottish winter?

A correctly designed heat pump can provide heating in cold weather. The important starting points are the property’s heat loss, insulation, radiator or underfloor-heating output and suitable controls. Have the whole system assessed rather than choosing equipment on floor area alone.

Energy Saving Trust

Should I repair my existing system or replace it?

Consider the diagnosis, repair price, parts availability, breakdown history and running costs together. A single fault does not automatically mean a replacement is necessary. Request a written comparison of the practical options before deciding.

Do I need planning permission for a heat pump in Glasgow?

Some domestic heat-pump installations in Scotland can use permitted development rights, subject to conditions. Cooling equipment, flats, listed buildings and conservation areas may need different checks. Confirm your proposal with the local planning authority before work begins.

Scottish Government planning guidance

Is there help towards the cost of a heat pump in Scotland?

Home Energy Scotland offers guidance on grants and loans for eligible home improvements. Eligibility, installer requirements and funding availability matter, so obtain advice and the required approval before committing to work. This website does not offer or approve finance.

Home Energy Scotland funding

What should I do if I smell gas?

Call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 in Great Britain. Do not use an online enquiry form for a suspected gas leak. Open doors and windows if safe, and follow the emergency service’s instructions.

HSE emergency guidance
