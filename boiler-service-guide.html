Your annual boiler service: what to ask and prepare

A little preparation can make a service appointment more useful and help you understand what was checked.

AeroFlux home comfort guide · 4 October 2026 · 3 min read

Choose the right engineer

For gas appliances, check the engineer’s Gas Safe registration and that their listed qualifications cover your appliance. HSE advises annual servicing of gas appliances by a suitably qualified professional.

Have the details ready

Locate the make and model, previous service record and any warranty paperwork. Make a note of intermittent faults, unusual noises or changes in heating performance. Do not remove appliance covers yourself.

Make the visit useful

Provide safe access to the appliance and its controls. Ask what the service includes and how additional repair work will be quoted. A service and a repair are different scopes of work.

Keep your records

Ask for a record of the completed service, relevant findings and recommended follow-up. Check your manufacturer’s maintenance conditions and keep the documentation with your appliance information.
